# Design direction
Sunlit Casablanca, approved by the user. Code-first implementation.
Operate mode: familiar navigation, search and filters above place results, details organized around deciding whether to visit.
First viewport: understated brand header, expressive teal/coral type paired with Moroccan photography, followed by the search controls. Ivory surfaces support daylight reading.
Signature: a warm photographic note inviting a real-world outing; the browsing cards keep cost and setting visible without opening details.
Palette: ivory #fbfaf6, deep teal #163a35, action teal #174e43, coral #bc4b2d, sage #e8ecdf. Typeface: self-hosted DM Sans.
Responsive: three listing columns on desktop; one readable photo-led column on mobile. Navigation collapses to an accessible menu.
Content: sample imagery and listings must be labeled. No invented ratings, visitor counts, offers or verification.
